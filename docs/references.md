## Regulatory and Policy Documents

### Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS)

1. **Basel III: The Liquidity Coverage Ratio and liquidity risk monitoring tools** (2013)
   - BCBS Publication No. 238
   - Defines LCR calculation methodology
   - Available: https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs238.htm

2. **Basel III: The Net Stable Funding Ratio** (2014)
   - BCBS Publication No. 295
   - NSFR framework and requirements
   - Available: https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d295.htm

3. **Monitoring tools for intraday liquidity management** (2013)
   - BCBS Publication No. 248
   - Intraday liquidity monitoring framework
   - Available: https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs248.htm

4. **Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management and Supervision** (2008)
   - BCBS Publication No. 144
   - Core principles for liquidity risk management
   - Available: https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs144.htm

### Central Banks and Regulators

5. **Federal Reserve Board (2014)**
   - "Liquidity Coverage Ratio: Liquidity Risk Measurement Standards"
   - Federal Register, Vol. 79, No. 197
   - US implementation of Basel III LCR

6. **European Banking Authority (2015)**
   - "Guidelines on LCR disclosure to complement the disclosure of liquidity risk management"
   - EBA/GL/2017/01
   - EU liquidity disclosure requirements

7. **Bank for International Settlements (2005)**
   - "New developments in large-value payment systems"
   - CPSS Publications No. 67
   - Payment system infrastructure analysis

## Academic Research Papers

### Liquidity Risk

8. **Brunnermeier, M.K. and Pedersen, L.H. (2009)**
   - "Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity"
   - *Review of Financial Studies*, 22(6), pp. 2201-2238
   - Seminal paper on liquidity spirals

9. **Tirole, J. (2011)**
   - "Illiquidity and All Its Friends"
   - *Journal of Economic Literature*, 49(2), pp. 287-325
   - Comprehensive survey of liquidity economics

10. **Diamond, D.W. and Dybvig, P.H. (1983)**
    - "Bank Runs, Deposit Insurance, and Liquidity"
    - *Journal of Political Economy*, 91(3), pp. 401-419
    - Classic model of bank runs

### Payment Systems

11. **Bech, M.L. and Soramäki, K. (2001)**
    - "Gridlock Resolution in Interbank Payment Systems"
    - Bank of Finland Discussion Paper No. 9/2001
    - Analysis of gridlock mechanisms

12. **Galbiati, M. and Soramäki, K. (2011)**
    - "An Agent-Based Model of Payment Systems"
    - *Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control*, 35(6), pp. 859-875
    - Agent-based payment system modeling

13. **Koponen, R. and Soramäki, K. (1998)**
    - "Intraday Liquidity Needs in a Modern Interbank Payment System"
    - Bank of Finland Studies E:14
    - Empirical study of liquidity demands

14. **Martin, A. and McAndrews, J. (2008)**
    - "Liquidity-Saving Mechanisms"
    - *Journal of Monetary Economics*, 55(3), pp. 554-567
    - Efficiency of payment queue management

### Financial Contagion and Systemic Risk

15. **Allen, F. and Gale, D. (2000)**
    - "Financial Contagion"
    - *Journal of Political Economy*, 108(1), pp. 1-33
    - Network effects in financial systems

16. **Upper, C. (2011)**
    - "Simulation Methods to Assess the Danger of Contagion in Interbank Markets"
    - *Journal of Financial Stability*, 7(3), pp. 111-125
    - Survey of contagion simulation approaches

17. **Gai, P. and Kapadia, S. (2010)**
    - "Contagion in Financial Networks"
    - *Proceedings of the Royal Society A*, 466(2120), pp. 2401-2423
    - Network topology and systemic risk
